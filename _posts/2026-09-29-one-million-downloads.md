ActivityWatch has passed **1 million downloads**. The desktop app's GitHub Releases alone now count over 1.18 million, and that doesn't include package managers, distro repositories, Google Play, or F-Droid. We didn't mark the moment when it happened, so this is us catching up.

  <p><small>Cumulative and weekly desktop downloads from GitHub Releases. Live chart on the <a href="/stats/">stats page</a>, along with users, stars, and Android numbers.</small></p>
</div>

The weekly rate has roughly quadrupled since 2023 and is now over 10,000 downloads a week.

ActivityWatch is free, open source, and stores your data on your own machine. It has been built mostly by volunteers, so a number like this means a lot to us. Thank you to every user, and to every contributor who has written code, watchers, translations, docs, or bug reports.

## What's next

The stable desktop release, v0.14, is what we're preparing now. On mobile, [ActivityWatch for Android 0.14 just went stable]({% post_url 2026-09-29-activitywatch-android-0-14-stable %}), with sync, alerts, and a large batch of crash fixes.
