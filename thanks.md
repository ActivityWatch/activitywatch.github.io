Thank you for supporting ActivityWatch. Your subscription is what keeps the project actively maintained, independent, and free for everyone.

**Nothing changes about how you use ActivityWatch.** It stays free, open source, and fully on your own machine — no account, no cloud, no tracking. You already had every feature; you're funding the tool you're already using so it stays healthy for the long term.

That's it. Head back to [ActivityWatch](/) and get on with your day.

*ActivityWatch will always be free and open-source. Your support makes that sustainable.*
